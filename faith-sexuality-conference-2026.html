Why This Conference

A conversation the Church can't sit out

1

The Reality

Across Kenya and the continent, questions of same-sex attraction and gender identity are reaching every church, family, school, and workplace — whether we've prepared for them or not.

2

The Need

Many believers are quietly wrestling with these questions. Pastors, parents, and leaders are asking how to respond with conviction and compassion at once — not one instead of the other.

3

The Response

This conference exists so the Church doesn't look away. Through careful biblical teaching and honest conversation, we want every participant to leave equipped to think biblically, love faithfully, and engage with wisdom.

"What should I do if my child comes out to me as gay?"

One of the questions we'll answer together
What We'll Cover

The questions we'll wrestle with

Five questions shaping the day's teaching and discussion.

  1. 01What is God's design and vision for human sexuality?
  2. 02Can a Christian wrestle with same-sex attraction or gender dysphoria?
  3. 03How can Christians uphold truth and grace when engaging with those experiencing sexual brokenness?
  4. 04What should I do if my child comes out to me as gay?
  5. 05How should Christians engage with the LGBT community?
